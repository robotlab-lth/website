The Robotlab at the Department of Computer Science is where the group's
experimental work happens. Equipment includes dual-arm and single-arm
manipulators, mobile platforms, autonomous model cars, and the sensing rigs used
in our perception projects.

The lab is used both for research and for project courses; students taking
Intelligent Autonomous Systems and Project in Computer Science run their
experiments here.
