## NLP
{: #nlp}

Natural language processing: parsing, semantic role labelling, word embeddings
and language models, and the use of language as an interface to robots.

## Machine learning
{: #machine-learning}

Learning from demonstration, reinforcement learning for manipulation, and
probabilistic modelling of perceptual outcomes.

## Robotics
{: #robotics}

Task-level programming, manipulation, mobile robots and autonomous model cars,
both indoors and outdoors.

## Computer Vision
{: #computer-vision}

Reliable perception for autonomous systems: object detection and pose
estimation, latent space learning and tracking, and adaptation of sensing to
the situation at hand.

## Human robot interaction
{: #human-robot-interaction}

Mixed-initiative interaction, instructing robots in natural language, and
shared situational awareness between human and robot teams.

## Knowledge representation
{: #knowledge-representation}

Ontologies and knowledge integration for robot systems, semantic maps and
reasoning about actions and change.

## Software
{: #software}

Software architectures, skill libraries and tooling that make robot systems
reusable across platforms and accessible to small and medium-sized enterprises.
