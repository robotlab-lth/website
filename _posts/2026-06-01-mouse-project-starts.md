---
title: "New project: MOUSE, multimodal perception for robots in adverse conditions"
date: 2026-06-01
---

A new four-year project has started in the group. **MOUSE — Multimodal
Multitask Learning for Reliable Robot Situational Awareness in Adverse
Environmental Conditions** runs from June 2026 to May 2030, with Eren Erdal
Aksoy as principal investigator.

Autonomous robots need robust perception and navigation in environments where
LiDAR, RADAR and RGB-D cameras degrade or fail. MOUSE is an adaptive multimodal
fusion framework that learns what each sensor is good for and selects the
configuration that suits the conditions at hand, learning several downstream
perception tasks jointly rather than one at a time.

See the [Projects page](/research/projects/) for the rest of the group's
current work.
