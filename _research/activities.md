Members of the group organise seminars, workshops and conferences, sit on
editorial boards and evaluation panels, examine theses elsewhere, and take part
in schools and media outreach. The
[Lund University Research Portal](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/organisations/robotics-and-semantic-systems/activities/)
records **42 such activities** for the group.

## Recent

### NextG2Com 3rd Partner Workshop

*16 April 2026 — organisation of workshop/seminar/course.*

Partner workshop of NEXTG2COM, the Vinnova competence centre in advanced
digitalisation. Organised by Maria Kihl, Per Runeson, T. Larsson and
S. Lönnqvist; **Ayesha Jena** took part as an invited speaker, alongside
K. Anderberg Halasz and N. Mazloum. Keynote and plenary speakers were A. Reial
and R. Lagerstedt.

### AI Lund lunch seminar: Sure, Your Algorithm Is Really Fast, But Is It Really Correct?

*15 April 2026 — organisation of public lecture/debate/seminar.*

An AI Lund lunch seminar organised by Jonas Wisbrant, with Jakob Nordström as
keynote speaker and **Jacek Malec** in the chair.

### WASP Workshop on Diversity and Inclusion in Academia

*19 September 2025 — organisation of public lecture/debate/seminar.*

Organised by Per Runeson within the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and
Software Program, with Amy Loutfi as session chair. **Ayesha Jena**
participated, together with Sylvia Schwaag Serger, Joachim Schnadt, Ekta Vats
and Sandra Di Rocco.
