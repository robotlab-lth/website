We supervise master theses across all of the group's research areas. Broad
directions we are currently interested in:

- Perception that adapts to the situation it is used in
- Task-level programming and skill reuse for industrial robots
- Mixed-initiative interaction between people and robot teams
- Language models as an interface to robot systems
- Knowledge representation for semantic maps

If one of these is close to something you want to work on, get in touch with the
researcher whose page mentions it.
