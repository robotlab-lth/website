---
title: "Simon Kristoffersson Lind defends thesis on uncertainty estimation for robotic vision"
date: 2026-01-15
---

Simon Kristoffersson Lind has completed his doctoral thesis (compilation),
*Uncertainty estimation for adaptable and reliable robotic vision*, at Lund
University.

The work runs alongside *GPify: leveraging the combined strength of normalizing
flow and softmax for an out-of-distribution aware confidence score*, written
with Rudolph Triebel and Volker Krueger and published open access in the
International Journal of Computer Vision (volume 134, article 185). The paper
combines normalizing flows with the softmax output of a classifier to produce a
confidence score that stays meaningful when the input falls outside the
distribution the network was trained on — the situation that makes perception
brittle on a robot operating in the world rather than on a benchmark.
